x86 emulator

This was catalogued as hindawiai/v86 (fork of copy/v86), plus js-dos and em-dosbox — and never mounted on the console. It is mounted here.

v86 is third-party (BSD). The guest is FreeDOS from copy.sh. Original Lekhak is DJGPP+UPX: you need DPMI. Binaries from hindawi3.zip:

lekhakx.exe · CWSDPMI.EXE · CMD.COM

PANINI Lekhak (LEKHAK.C port, not the EXE) is on the console: type lekhak. The EXE is the 2004 binary; the port is the source in this language.
